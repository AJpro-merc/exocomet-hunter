    Reading this table honestly¶

    Two subsystems are genuinely close to mature by this project's own bar: Data Acquisition and Database/Ledger, both with real fixes validated against real failures they actually hit in production-like conditions. Two are genuinely near-zero: Vetting (literally an empty package) and the Classifier (written but with no confirmed real evaluation and no accumulated training data). The remainder sit in a realistic middle: working code, incomplete test/validation coverage, and at least one open, named bug or gap each. This distribution — strong infrastructure, weak scientific rigor layer — matches the plain-language summary at the top of docs/NEXT_STEPS.md and is not an artifact of unusually critical scoring; it is a direct read of the evidence gathered in sections 43–72 above.
