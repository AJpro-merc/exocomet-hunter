    15. Model Fitting¶

    Status: reflects repo state as of 2026-09-16, commit 01c2051. IMPLEMENTED / TESTED (tests/unit/test_model_comparison.py exists). The known open issue with fit stability on shallow events (§15.5) is UNVERIFIED as a fixed bug — it is an open item per docs/NEXT_STEPS.md.

    15.1 Module and rationale¶

    src/exocomet/detect/model_comparison.py fits two competing parametric profiles to each candidate event and compares them by BIC. The module docstring explains why this exists alongside the asymmetry ratios in detect/asymmetry.py: those ratios "are interpretable but non-parametric — they compress a whole event into two numbers and say nothing about how well any physical shape actually describes the data." The approach is attributed to Kennedy et al. (2019).

    15.2 The two profiles — exact mathematical form as implemented¶

    Symmetric (null hypothesis) — symmetric_profile(t, t0, depth, width, baseline) (lines 44–63):

    flux(t) = baseline - depth * exp(-0.5 * ((t - t0) / width)^2)

    A single Gaussian dip, parameterised by centre t0, depth, standard deviation width (days), and out-of-dip baseline. width is floored at _MIN_WIDTH_DAYS = 1e-4 days to prevent the optimiser collapsing the profile onto a single cadence.

    Comet — comet_profile(t, t0, depth, ingress_width, egress_tau, baseline) (lines 66–95):

    for t <= t0:  flux(t) = baseline - depth * exp(-0.5 * ((t - t0) / ingress_width)^2)
for t >  t0:  flux(t) = baseline - depth * exp(-(t - t0) / egress_tau)

    A Gaussian ingress (standard deviation ingress_width) joined continuously at the minimum to an exponential egress (e-folding timescale egress_tau) — both halves reach exactly baseline - depth at t0. Both ingress_width and egress_tau are floored at _MIN_WIDTH_DAYS. This is not a generic "Gaussian plus exponential" mixture; it is precisely this one-sided-Gaussian / one-sided-exponential join, five free parameters total (t0, depth, ingress_width, egress_tau, baseline) versus the symmetric profile's four (t0, depth, width, baseline).

    15.3 Fitting procedure¶

    _fit(name, model, x0, bounds, t, y, yerr) -> ModelFit (lines 102–138) — runs scipy.optimize.least_squares (method "trf", max_nfev=2000) on error-weighted residuals (model(t, *params) - y) / yerr, bounded. On ValueError/RuntimeError (pathological windows), it degrades to converged=False, chi2=inf, params left at the initial guess — it does not raise.

    compare_models(time, flux, flux_err, event, baseline_level, padding_factor=1.5, delta_bic_threshold=10.0) -> ModelComparison | None (lines 141–221):

    1. Fitting region: the detected event.window widened by padding_factor (default 1.5×) on both sides, computed as pad = 0.5 * span * (padding_factor - 1.0) around the window's own duration — "so that the recovering tail ... is included rather than truncated," since the detected window was drawn where the dip exceeds the 4σ significance threshold, which is by construction narrower than the full event.
    2. If fewer than 8 cadences fall in the padded window, returns None (five free parameters for the comet model cannot be meaningfully constrained by fewer than 8 points).
    3. flux_err values <= 0 are replaced with 1.0 (np.where(flux_err[mask] > 0, flux_err[mask], 1.0)) to avoid a zero-division in the residual weighting.
    4. Initial guesses: t0 = event.t_min, depth0 = max(event.depth, 1e-9), width0 = max(span / 4.0, 1e-3). Both profiles are seeded from the same depth0/width0.
    5. Bounds keep t0 within the padded window, depth within [0, 10×depth0], widths/tau within [1e-4, span] (symmetric) or [1e-4, 10×span] (comet's egress_tau, which may need to extend well past the window), and baseline within ±10×depth0 of the seed.
    6. Returns ModelComparison(symmetric=..., comet=..., delta_bic_threshold=...) — the threshold is threaded through from the caller so ModelComparison.favors_comet and the flagging decision in detect/scoring.py share one configured source of truth rather than two independently hardcoded values.

    15.4 BIC and derived quantities (core/types.py)¶

    ModelFit.bic (lines 205–214): chi2 + n_params * ln(n_points) if converged and n_points > 0, else inf. Lower is better; the n_params * ln(n_points) term penalises the comet model's extra free parameter.

    ModelComparison.delta_bic (lines 228–236): BIC(symmetric) - BIC(comet), positive favours the comet profile; returns -inf if the raw difference is non-finite (e.g. because one side failed to converge).

    ModelComparison.favors_comet (lines 238–241): delta_bic > delta_bic_threshold.

    ModelComparison.tau_over_sigma (lines 243–268): comet.params["egress_tau"] / comet.params["ingress_width"], nan if either is missing/non-finite or the ingress width is <= 0. The property's own docstring derives exactly why this, not a contour-based ratio, is the primary shape statistic — see 16_statistics.md and research log 003 for the full derivation; in short, a contour measured at depth fraction f compares σ√(2 ln(1/f)) against τ ln(1/f), which at the half-depth contour (f=0.5) gives 1.177σ vs 0.693τ and flips sign at τ/σ ≈ 1.70 even while the tail is unambiguously longer — a distortion tau_over_sigma, derived from the whole fitted profile, does not share.

    15.5 Fit stability — a real, unresolved finding (H2, research log 006)¶

    The B1 depth-offset investigation (docs/research_log/006-depth-offset-investigation.md) tested the comet model's fitted depth (ModelComparison.comet.params["depth"]) against the published KIC 3542116 values as one of three hypotheses for a separate depth-measurement bias, and found the fit itself to be unreliable on shallow events: median ratio 1.316× against published depth with a standard deviation of 1.009 — "essentially as large as the mean." Individual ratios ranged from 0.99× (D1176) to 3.47× (D140 — a 491 ppm published dip fit at 1704 ppm). The research log's own conclusion: "The comet-profile fit is diverging badly for at least the shallow events. This is very likely a separate, real bug in detect/model_comparison.py's fit — worth its own investigation." docs/NEXT_STEPS.md lists this as open item #4 under "FIRST THING NEXT SESSION" as of 2026-09-16 (working tree). Status: PARTIAL / UNVERIFIED as of this document — the underlying fit instability has been observed and reported but not root-caused or fixed. No change has been made to compare_models or _fit in response to this finding as of commit 01c2051 or the current working tree.

    Because tau_over_sigma is computed from the same comet fit whose depth parameter is shown above to diverge on shallow events, this finding is a caution against over-trusting tau_over_sigma/delta_bic for the shallowest events specifically — though no quantitative check of tau_over_sigma's own stability under the same divergence was performed in log 006 (it tested depth only). This document does not extend that claim further than the source states.
